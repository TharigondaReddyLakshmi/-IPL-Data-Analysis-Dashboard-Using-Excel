# -IPL-Data-Analysis-Dashboard-Using-Excel
 IPL Data Analysis Dashboard (2008 - 2025)  An interactive, data-driven Excel dashboad to analyze IPL cricket metrics spanning from 2008 to 2025. This dashboard transforms historical cricket datasets into high-level strategic visualizations, identifying winning trends, toss impacts, team dominances, and standout player accomplishments.


# 📝 Dashboard Description

This analytics project evaluates seasonal patterns and performance benchmarks for all IPL franchises across multiple tournament iterations. Designed with a clean aesthetic and clear layout, the interface features dynamic filtering capabilities that isolate individual years to dynamically track changing league behaviors, structural shifting of venue favorability, and evolving match dynamics.

# 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) Used

The dashboard monitors macro-level tournament outcomes through distinct high-level context blocks:

• Year Selector: Filters individual seasons from IPL-2008 up to IPL-2025 to dynamically update all matching on-screen visualizations.

• Winner Badge: Displays the absolute champion franchise of the selected season (e.g., Rajasthan Royals for 2008).

• Runner-Up Badge: Identifies the second-place franchise of that season (e.g., Chennai Super Kings for 2008).

• Man of the Season: Recognizes the most valuable individual player performance of that season (e.g., Shane Watson for 2008).

# 📉 Charts and Visual Elements Used

The layout incorporates distinct visualization styles to summarize matches accurately:

• Clustered Column Chart (Matches Won by Team WRT Bat First & Bowl First): Evaluates franchise success rates based on match conditions. It tracks total matches won when batting first versus chasing (bowling first) across all teams, led by Mumbai Indians and Chennai Super Kings.

• Donut Chart (Toss Decision Based Winning %): Displays a macro-level percentage split comparing how often teams win after choosing to field (65%) versus choosing to bat (35%).

• Bidirectional / Stacked Horizontal Bar Chart (Top 10 Venues with Most Matches): Compares the top 10 iconic stadiums (e.g., Eden Gardens, Wankhede, M Chinnaswamy) based on total match volumes and splits their outcomes according to whether the winning team batted first or fielded first.

• Treemap Chart (No. of Trophies with Each Team): A color-blocked proportional map displaying historic championship distribution, charting league-leading totals for heavy hitters like Mumbai Indians, Chennai Super Kings, and Kolkata Knight Riders.

• Column Chart (No. of MOM): A ranked column chart highlighting the league's top individual match-winners based on total Man of the Match awards earned, spearheaded by AB de Villiers (25) and Chris Gayle (22).

• Grid Slicers: A segmented selection grid enabling instant multi-year slicing spanning two decades of IPL history.

# 🎯 Conclusion & Key Takeaways

• Chasing Dominance: Quick-commerce and modern analytical cricket show a heavy preference for chasing. The data demonstrates a massive bias toward fielding first, commanding a 65% toss-to-win ratio globally.

• Consistent Powerhouses: Historically, franchises like Mumbai Indians and Chennai Super Kings lead the pack not only in total volume match wins but also dominate the ultimate metric—championship trophies.

• Venue Bias: Major tracks like M Chinnaswamy Stadium skew heavily toward chasing teams due to favorable ground dimensions and outfield speeds, whereas grounds like Eden Gardens present a relatively more balanced platform for target defenders.
